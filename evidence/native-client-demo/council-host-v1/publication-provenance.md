# Council publication boundary

The raw Grok and Claude output streams, their stderr files and the transient prepared receipt for this host-edition review are privately retained and excluded from Git publication. They were available for the completed independent local integrity audit. Their SHA-256 values remain in the structured receipts.

A fresh clone includes the frozen reviewed packet, capture provenance, launch records, prompt, human-readable model reviews, structured council/integrity receipts and adopted decisions. Those records bind the original candidate and the locally audited outputs. A clone cannot independently repeat raw-stream parsing without the private originals; published hashes do not substitute for those bytes.

This exclusion applies only to the host-edition files listed in publication-provenance.json. Earlier accepted council artifacts remain unchanged. No private files were deleted and no additional inference was started.
